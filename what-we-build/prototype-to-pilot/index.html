Where you are

You have a prototype that works, yours or one we built with you. Before more units, it needs testing, design-for-manufacturing fixes and documentation.

What you have

  • A working prototype
  • Its design files and BOM, if you have them
  • How it will be used, and by whom

What we do at this stage

  • Power-on, function, communication and structural tests
  • First-article inspection and design-for-manufacturing fixes
  • An issue log, kept open until every item is closed
  • Agree with you on fixtures, how each unit is tested and recorded, and how units are packed
  • Build the pilot batch once you approve the design

What you get

  • Test checklist, test report and test videos
  • Production files
  • Pilot units, each tested before it is packed
  • A batch record

Services that apply

  • Testing, quality & documentation: tests, first-article inspection, production files
  • PCB / PCBA & component sourcing: boards, BOM, parts and approved alternatives
  • Enclosure & product integration: a product body that can be built and serviced
  • Pilot & small-batch assembly: tested units, batch record, packed shipment
