Indoor delivery robot

An indoor delivery robot for moving materials in factories, warehouses, labs and other indoor spaces. It follows a person wearing a UWB tag at a set distance, or can be driven with a handheld remote. Ultrasonic sensors stop it from running into obstacles, and it carries up to 200 kg in a lidded aluminium-frame box on four air-filled wheels.

Tech stack

Main controller
ESP32 running FreeRTOS
Positioning
UWB modules front and rear (UART); follow distance 1–7 m, adjustable
Obstacle sensing
4 ultrasonic sensors (RS485) and 6-axis IMU, multi-sensor fusion
Camera
1080p camera with video streaming and recording
Drive
4WD differential drive, in-place turning, 10-inch air-filled tires, PID / S-curve motor control
Power
24 V 20 Ah battery with BMS; ≈4 h charge; up to 15 km per charge
Load & speed
≤200 kg payload, ≈30 kg empty, up to 5 km/h, ≤10° slope, 5 cm obstacles
Remote
Handheld remote, ≤40 m range; optional 4G remote monitoring

What we built

  • Mechanical design: aluminium-frame body, lidded cargo box, 4WD chassis
  • Control hardware: ESP32 main board, motor drivers, power and BMS, sensor wiring
  • Embedded software: UWB follow algorithm, remote control, obstacle-avoidance logic, motor control
  • Status display (voltage, distance, sensor data) and video streaming

Development time
