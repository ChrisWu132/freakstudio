Laser weeding robot

An agricultural service robot for row weeding and precision cutting with a high-power laser array. A four-wheel mobile base carries a laser beam that tilts on a single axis. An operator drives it remotely and aims through live video, and multi-channel safety interlocks guard the lasers. The system runs on ROS for mobility, control and teleoperation.

Tech stack

Mobile base
4-wheel differential drive platform, fabricated body and protective enclosure
Laser system
High-power laser modules in groups, on a mounting beam with a single-axis pitch mechanism
Controller
Embedded controller with onboard control and safety logic; power distribution system
Camera & link
Camera with live 4G video transmission; wireless video link
Software
ROS-based mobility and teleoperation framework; chassis and pitch-axis control; laser enable, selection and firing logic
Safety
Multi-channel safety interlocks, dual-enable firing, fault handling and logging
Operator station
Remote control station for teleoperation and monitoring

What we built

  • Mechanical design, fabrication and assembly of the base, enclosure, laser beam and pitch mechanism
  • Hardware integration and power architecture, including laser grouping/switching and safety interlock circuitry
  • Embedded device-side software: motion, pitch-axis and laser firing logic, status monitoring and logging
  • Remote control station and video link
  • ROS-based system integration and teleoperation framework

Main challenges

  • Mechanical structure, fabrication and assembly
  • Laser array mounting and pitch-angle control
  • Remote manual aiming and multi-channel safety interlocks
  • ROS integration for mobility, control and remote operation
