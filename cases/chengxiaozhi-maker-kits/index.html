ChengXiaoZhi youth maker kits

ChengXiaoZhi is a series of six creative electronics kits for youth STEAM education and corporate gifts. Each kit runs on a Raspberry Pi RP2040, pairs a practical sensor with local processing and a display, and comes in a clear acrylic case with 120 minutes of teaching material. Name, logo, PCB silkscreen and packaging can be customized.

Tech stack

Main controller
Raspberry Pi RP2040
Kit 1 – UV detector
GUVA-S12SD UV sensor, 7-segment display
Kit 2 – Health monitor
Body temperature, heart rate and SpO2 sensing, OLED display
Kit 3 – Touch & sound
Vibration sensor, MP3 module
Kit 4 – Audio visualizer
MAX9814 microphone, 16-LED bar
Kit 5 – Light balance
Ambient light sensor, 16-segment LED bar
Kit 6 – Plant care
Soil moisture sensor, LED indicator, buzzer

What we built

  • Hardware design: schematic, PCB, component selection
  • Firmware for sensors, local processing and displays
  • Acrylic case and structural design
  • Assembly guide and tutorial video
  • 120-minute teaching materials per kit
  • Logo, colour and packaging customization

Development time

about 30 days for 6 kits (about 10 days per kit)
