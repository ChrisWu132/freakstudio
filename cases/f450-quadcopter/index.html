F450 quadcopter platform

A teaching and research quadcopter built on the F450 frame and the open-source APM flight controller. The team integrates the frame, flight controller, GPS, motors and power system, then assembles, tests and tunes it into a stable, extensible platform for education, prototype development and algorithm validation. Optional work adds autonomous flight such as waypoint navigation, position hold and automatic takeoff and landing.

Tech stack

Frame
F450 glass-fibre nylon frame
Flight controller
APM (open source)
GPS
u-blox NEO-M8N module
Propulsion
Brushless motors, ESCs, propellers
Power
LiPo battery
Radio
FlySky / Radiolink remote control
Ground station
Mission Planner

What we built

  • F450 frame integration and optimization
  • Flight controller setup, power distribution, wiring and power system
  • Motor, ESC, propeller, GPS and receiver integration
  • 3D-printed mounting parts where needed
  • Full assembly, flight testing and parameter tuning
  • Optional: autonomous waypoint flight, position hold, auto takeoff and landing, path planning and ground-station integration
