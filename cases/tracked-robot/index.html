Swing-arm tracked robot.

A tracked robot chassis with swing-arm tracks and a PTZ camera. Our engineers in Shenzhen designed and built it, from board and firmware to enclosure and packaging, and delivered the batch.

Tech stack

Chassis
Articulated tracked chassis, independent left/right tracks, swing-arm track modules, custom metal structure
Drive
High-torque motors and rugged drive system
Main controller
Embedded controller board (motion control, I/O, communication)
Camera
Pan-tilt (PTZ) camera, 360° view, auxiliary lighting
Link
4G low-latency wireless link for remote control, HD video and telemetry
Power & safety
Power management, emergency stop, fault detection and alarm reporting
Operator software
PC / mobile control interface with live video, telemetry and status (battery, temperature, signal)

What we built

  • Mechanical design of the articulated chassis, plus manufacturing and assembly
  • Hardware design: motor control, power system, main controller, sensors, 4G communication, video transmission
  • Embedded software: real-time motion control and motor coordination, swing-arm and PTZ camera control, sensor monitoring
  • Remote control software for PC and mobile: driving, camera operation, telemetry, manual and assisted modes
