RM competition robot: omni-wheel chassis

An omni-wheel competition robot built to maneuver quickly in tight spaces. It also works as a base for teaching and further development. The team did the complete hardware, the chassis and gimbal mechanics, and the embedded software.

Custom chassis and gimbal

What we built

  • Mechanical design of chassis and gimbal
