Eight-thruster underwater ROV with arm

A remote-operated underwater vehicle for inspection, object handling, scientific research and environmental monitoring. Eight thrusters give it full six-axis control, and a multi-joint arm at the front grasps and samples. The modular design accepts sonar, water samplers and other tools.

Tech stack

Main controller
STM32 / Pixhawk / industrial PC (slide lists all three)
Propulsion
8 thrusters, 8 ESCs; surge, sway, heave, roll, pitch and yaw control
Manipulator
4–6 DOF arm (servo / actuator control)
Sensors
IMU and depth sensor; optional sonar, USBL or vision positioning
Camera
1080p camera with high-power LED lighting
Link
Tether with surface 4G gateway for real-time video, control and data
Ground station
Based on QGroundControl or custom software: live video, recording, sensor display, mission planning and logging

Development time

2 months
