Desk AI companion

A small character-shaped desk device that connects to a computer over Bluetooth and shows the live status of an AI coding agent on its screen, with voice alerts when tasks change. It was built for developers, educators and makers, and comes with swappable 3D-printed accessories such as glasses, ears and hats. A second variant in a white enclosure was also prototyped.

Tech stack

Connectivity
BLE 5.0
Screen UI
LVGL
Firmware
MicroPython / Arduino-style embedded firmware
Protocol
Custom protocol encoding and decoding
Audio
Microphone input, speaker output, voice playback
Power
Li-ion battery with charging and protection
Desktop side
Desktop daemon / tool integration
