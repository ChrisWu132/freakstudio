Bionic Hexapod Robot

A multi-terrain six-legged robot for education, research and creative projects. It runs on an STM32 with 18 high-torque servos and can walk, turn, crab-walk, dance and run 24 preset actions. A dedicated Android app and a wireless remote control it.

Tech stack

Main controller
STM32F407 (STM32F4 series)
Actuators
18 high-torque metal servos; 24 servo channels on the board
Sensors
MPU6050 IMU; optional radar / camera
Control
Bluetooth Android app and wireless remote
Power
3S Li-ion battery (11.1 V) with voltage monitoring
Firmware
Inverse kinematics, multi-gait algorithms with PID control, IMU attitude estimation, 24 preset actions
Structure
3D-printed modular frame

What we built

  • Mechanical design of the six-leg, 18-DOF frame
  • PCB design with 24 servo interfaces
  • Wireless control and battery voltage monitoring
  • Embedded software: gait control, sensor processing, 24 preset actions
  • Android app for remote control, mode switching, action selection and parameter tuning

Development time

20 days
