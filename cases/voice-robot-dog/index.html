Offline voice-controlled robot dog

A compact four-legged robot that wakes up and follows voice commands offline, with no cloud connection. It walks using several servos, shows expressions on an OLED face and talks back through a built-in speaker. The prototype combines a 3D-printed body, a custom controller board and embedded firmware, aimed at educational robotics kits and STEM demos.
