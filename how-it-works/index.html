How a project moves.

Seven steps from first message to repeat production. One Anvol engineering lead is your contact throughout, and you confirm each step before the next one starts.

1. Project brief

You provide
A description, files or photos, quantity and target date.
Anvol does
Reads it and asks for what is missing.
You get
A reply on WhatsApp or email.
Payment
None.

2. NDA and information review

You provide
Your files and any samples.
Anvol does
Signs an NDA before opening any file, then reviews everything you sent.
You get
A list of missing information.
Payment
None.

3. Feasibility and risk assessment

Anvol does
Checks architecture, part availability, mechanical fit and how the product will be tested.
You get
A risk list and the recommended next step. Complex projects can take a detailed engineering review first.
Payment
None for the quick check. A detailed review is scoped separately.

4. Scope, estimate and proposal

You get
A written scope: deliverables, acceptance criteria and price.
You confirm
Scope and price, then sign. Contract with Anvol's US entity in USD, or with the partner factory directly plus an Anvol management fee.
Payment
A deposit starts the work.

5. Engineering and prototype build

Anvol does
PCB, firmware, enclosure and sourcing, built into the first units.
You get
Design files and working prototypes.
You confirm
The prototype, or the changes it needs.
Payment
Milestone payment as set in the contract.

6. Validation and pilot batch

Anvol does
Tests with you, revises, then builds a pilot batch.
You get
Test checklist and report, pilot units.
You confirm
The design is approved before any larger run.
Payment
Milestone payment as set in the contract.

7. Repeat production and handover

Anvol does
Purchasing, assembly, inspection and packaging on partner lines.
You get
Tested units and the production files for your design.
You confirm
Acceptance of each batch.
Payment
Per batch.

Ownership and changes

Your design
You own the project-specific design files, drawings and tooling.
Our tools
Anvol's general tools and libraries stay ours; third-party and open-source parts keep their licenses.
Part substitutions
Need your approval.
Scope changes
Major changes are re-quoted, with a new timeline, before work continues.
