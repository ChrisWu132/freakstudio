Our experience

Built for our own hardware business

Anvol uses this workflow to find customers for its own hardware-engineering business. Each lead keeps its sources; each reply keeps its context.

What you can inspect

Buyer-fit evidence, reviewed copy, send records, bounce reasons and replies awaiting action. Start with a defined pilot and use the resulting conversations to decide whether to expand.

A reply is not purchase intent. SMTP acceptance is not inbox delivery. We do not promise a reply rate or order count.

Process

How it works

Step 01

Find your buyers

We research matching companies using your product line and public buying evidence, then check website contacts and email sources. Pilot size depends on the qualified contacts we actually find.

Step 02

One email per company

Every email is written from that company's own website — what they sell, what they build. Not a template. Each one is reviewed before sending.

Step 03

Identified senders

We use assigned mailboxes and daily limits. Supplier lists use public individual work contacts and exclude role addresses such as sales@ and info@.

Step 04

Work every reply

Replies land in our inbox. We triage, answer questions, send your quotes and samples, and follow up in US business hours — until it's an order or a clear no. The founder reads every reply.

✓

Why email works

Email is how US B2B buyers actually evaluate new suppliers. But it only works if it reaches the inbox, reads like a person wrote it, and gets a fast, competent reply in their time zone.

Infrastructure

Under the hood

Every component exists because a shortcut we tried earlier broke something.

01

Mailbox pool

We use a company inbox and campaign-specific mailbox pools, with an identified sender, account ownership and daily volume records.

02

Authentication & self-check

We check email configuration and test sending and receiving. Provider acceptance does not prove inbox delivery; later bounces are recorded too.

03

Warm-up & volume ladder

Each campaign has daily limits and assigned accounts. Volumes are adjusted using actual bounce results, starting with small batches and investigating persistent rejection.

04

Reputation protection

Before sending, we check opt-outs and contact history. Feedback returns to the queue: invalid addresses are suppressed and policy rejections are investigated separately.

05

Recipient discovery & rules

We find companies using product and public import evidence, then look for individual work emails on their websites and check MX records. A public email and valid MX do not guarantee delivery.

06

Writing & reply handling

A frontier language model drafts one email per company from its website. A QA gate checks facts and banned phrases before queuing. Replies thread in one conversation, auto-classified, then answered by a person in US hours. Founder reads all.
