How a project moves.

  1. Project brief

    You send what you have: idea, board, files or a sample.

  2. NDA and review

    We sign an NDA, then read every file you sent.

  3. Feasibility and risks

    We list what is missing and what could block the build.

  4. Scope and estimate

    A written scope and quote. A deposit starts the work.

  5. Engineering and prototype

    PCB, firmware and enclosure, built into first units.

  6. Validation and pilot

    We test with you and revise before a pilot batch.

  7. Production and handover

    Repeat batches, plus the files you need to own the design.
