How it works

Four steps, one point of contact

1

Tell us your product

Send drawings, a BOM or just a sketch. NDA first, always.

2

We match factories & quote

Three vetted options with the real factory quote, side by side.

3

Prototype and validate

Samples, DFM changes and certification testing before tooling.

4

Mass production with QC

On-site inspection each run, then freight and customs handled.

Every stage leaves a paper trail. You get:

DFM report

Manufacturability review with cost-down items, tolerance risks and component alternatives — before tooling money is spent.

AQL inspection report

In-line and pre-shipment checks against an agreed AQL, with photos of every defect found and how it was resolved.

Factory audit scorecard

How each shortlisted factory scored on quality systems, capacity, tooling and financial health — before you choose.
