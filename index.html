Products we designed and built

24-channel logic analyzer

100 MHz sampling, 3.3–5 V levels, sigrok/PulseView compatible. Our own board, enclosure, silkscreen and retail carton — a full box build, not just a PCBA.

Flat-frame underwater ROV

Sealed pressure housing, four thrusters, manipulator arm, tether and ground-station software. STM32 control board and firmware in house — including the leak testing.

Swing-arm tracked robot

Welded and machined steel chassis, dual swing-arm tracks, PTZ camera, e-stop. Mechanical fabrication and electronics integrated as one build.

Lidar line-following car

Custom control board with OLED, lidar obstacle avoidance and following, magnetic line tracking, phone app. Board, firmware and app all ours.

Large ROS inspection platform

Mecanum wheels, sprung suspension, extruded-aluminium frame and acrylic body, ROS stack on top. Built at a size where tolerances and welding actually matter.

Wheel-legged inspection robot

Wheeled drive with leg-linkage posture control — ride height, shoulder shake, jump — plus a WiFi FPV camera for remote inspection.

For the supply chain

Work with us, not around us

We build hardware. You sell parts, run tests, make cables, or ship products. When your work touches ours, here is how we fit together.

Start with an NDA →

Referral

Your customer picks a component and then hits an engineering wall. Send it to us. We handle schematic, PCB, firmware, prototype, pilot run. Your account stays yours. Confidentiality is default. Referral terms agreed before we start.

Handoff

You do testing, certification, cable, harness, displays, or mechanical. We do PCB, firmware, and prototype. We hand off clean files before your step or pick up after it.

Overflow

Your engineering team is full. A board revision, a variant, a second source, a prototype with no calendar slot. We run it under your specs. Your design stays yours.

Something not covered? Ask us directly →

Tell us what your people keep asking you for, then post it when there is something to show them. You do not design, quote, source, inspect or ship anything — our engineers do that, in Shenzhen, and you sign off at each step.

No — that is who this is for. Most of what we build starts as a sketch, a photo or a sentence. We turn it into drawings, a bill of materials and a sample you can hold, and we explain each decision in plain words before you approve it.

We ask what your people ask you for, what you have already tried, and what stopped you. If there is a product in it, we tell you what it would take — in plain numbers — before you decide anything. No slides, no pitch.

You do. We sign a bilingual NDA before we look at any file, and a separate NNN agreement with every factory we approach. Tooling and drawings stay registered to you, not to the factory, and your name goes on the product.

Two ways, and we tell you which one fits before you commit. Managed: you contract and pay the factory directly, we invoice a separate management fee, and you see every line of the factory quote. Turnkey: you contract with our US company in USD, we buy from the factory and invoice you one landed price. Either way terms are milestone-based — for example 30% at tooling, 70% before shipment.

Typical first runs are 500–2,000 units. For simpler mechanical parts we can start lower; for custom electronics the component MOQs usually set the floor, and we tell you where that lands before you commit.

Functional samples in two to three weeks, iterated until the spec is settled — most projects run two or three rounds. Production scheduling depends on the product, and you get the date in writing before anything is ordered.

Robotics, industrial IoT, test and measurement instruments, consumer electronics and smart home — the categories our own products sit in. If your product needs a process we haven't run before, we'll tell you that instead of quoting it.

We run in-line and pre-shipment inspections ourselves, on site, with photo reports against an agreed AQL. We then handle export documents and freight booking. Shipping terms are your call: FOB Shenzhen, where you clear customs and stay the importer of record, or delivered to your door, where we do.
