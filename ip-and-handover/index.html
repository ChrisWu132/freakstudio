What the NDA covers, who owns what at the end of a project, and the files, code and documents you take with you.

NDA

Before any file
We sign an NDA before we open anything you send.
Both ways
It is mutual: it covers what you send us and what we send you.
Partner lines
We share your files with a partner line only to quote or build, and only under a written confidentiality obligation at least as protective as your NDA.

Who owns what

Your design
You own the project-specific design files, drawings and tooling.
Our tools
Anvol's general tools and libraries stay ours.
Third-party parts
Third-party and open-source components keep their original licenses.

What you receive

Electronics
Schematic, PCB files, Gerbers, BOM with sources.
Firmware
Firmware source, factory test program, protocol and API notes.
Mechanical
Enclosure CAD files.
Testing
Test checklist, test report, test videos.
Production
Production files, and a batch record for each batch.
When
Design files come with the working prototypes; production files come with the tested units.
Which of these
The ones for the services in your written scope, which lists every deliverable before work starts.
