Sourcing

Scope
We buy every part, including older and hard-to-find chips.
With your quote
A full bill of materials, with a source for each part, costed from real parts and supplier quotes and checked by an engineer.
Alternatives
BOM cleanup includes approved alternative parts.
Partner lines
Production runs on partner lines. You see each line's type and region, not its name.
Contract
Your contract is with Anvol, Inc., not with a factory.

Testing

Prototypes
Power-on, function and communication tests, and structural checks.
Before a batch
First-article inspection and design-for-manufacturing fixes.
Before a run
We agree with you on fixtures, how each unit is tested and recorded, and how units are packed.
Every unit
Tested before it is packed, against the targets from the sample rounds.
Issues
Logged, and the log stays open until every item is closed.
You receive
Test checklist, test report and test videos; a batch record with each batch.

Who is responsible for what

Anvol
Engineering, sourcing, testing and managing production, within the written scope. One Anvol engineering lead is your contact throughout.
Partner lines
Fabrication and assembly runs, ordered and managed by Anvol.
You
Confirm the written scope and price, approve the prototype and the design before any larger run, and accept each batch.
Test labs
Certification testing such as FCC or CE. Outside our standard scope; quoted separately when a product needs it.
Shipping and customs
Usually arranged by you once units leave the factory. Import duties are yours.

Change control

At the start

Part substitutions
Need your approval before the part is used.
Scope changes
Major changes are re-quoted, with a new timeline, before work continues.
