Packed, recorded and ready to ship.

Each tested unit is packed with what goes in the box, protected for shipping, and listed in the batch record.

Who this is for

Teams whose batch is being built with us and who want it to leave packed and documented: retail boxes, classroom kits, or units going to a pilot.

Typical starting points

  • A batch being built on our pilot and small-batch service
  • A product that needs its first packaging
  • A kit whose parts need to be grouped and boxed

What Anvol does

  • Packaging design, including your logo and colours
  • Kitting: the unit, accessories, cables and printed guides in one box
  • Shipping protection
  • Packing each tested unit
  • Batch record
  • Shipping preparation: a packed shipment ready for your freight forwarder
  • Final assembly in the US: available on request, scoped per project

Deliverables

  • Packed shipment
  • Batch record
  • Packaging design, when it is in the scope

Typical inputs

  • What goes in the box
  • Logo, artwork or brand colours
  • Quantity
  • Where the batch goes and who ships it

Timeline and cost drivers

Timeline
Packing is agreed before the run and done after the units pass their tests; the date goes in the written scope.
Cost depends on
Packaging, what goes in the box, and quantity.

What is excluded

  • Shipping, customs clearance and import duties. Usually arranged by you once units leave the factory; duties are yours.
  • Final assembly in the US as a standard service. It is available only on request and scoped per project.
  • Certification testing (FCC/CE). It is done by a test lab and quoted separately when a product needs it.
  • Anything outside the written scope. Major scope changes are re-quoted, with a new timeline, before work continues.
