Working boards need a product body.

We design the enclosure around your boards, battery and controls, and make it in a way that can be assembled, serviced and shipped.

Who this is for

Working boards that need a product body, or a 3D-printed prototype that has to become something that can be built in batches.

Typical starting points

  • Bare boards working on the bench
  • A 3D-printed prototype you made yourself
  • Existing CAD that needs to fit new electronics or a different process

What Anvol does

  • 3D printing
  • CNC
  • Injection-molding preparation
  • Protection for the electronics
  • Heat
  • Battery and cable space
  • Screens, buttons, knobs and sliders
  • Shipping protection
  • Assembly and service access

Deliverables

  • CAD files
  • Printed or machined enclosures
  • Assembled units

Typical inputs

  • Board dimensions
  • CAD, if any
  • Products whose look or feel you like

Timeline and cost drivers

Timeline

What is excluded

  • Certification testing (FCC/CE). It is done by a test lab and quoted separately when a product needs it.
  • Anything outside the written scope. Major scope changes are re-quoted, with a new timeline, before work continues.
